Recipe for Lemon Grass Chicken

Decription

Lemongrass Chicken is a popular Southeast Asian dish made with tender chicken marinated or cooked in a fragrant blend of minced fresh lemongrass, garlic, fish sauce, and sugar.

Ingredients

  • Chicken: 1 to 2 pounds of boneless, skinless chicken thighs or breasts (thighs stay juicier)
  • Lemongrass: 2 to 4 stalks, tender white inner parts finely chopped or minced
  • Aromatics: 3 to4 cloves of garlic and 1 small shallot or green onion, minced
  • Sauces and Seasonings: 2 tablespoons fish sauce, 1 tablespoon soy sauce, and 1 to 2 tablespoons brown sugar or honey
  • Acid and Oil: 1 to 2 tablespoons fish sauce, 1 tablespoons neutral cooking oil (like canola or avocado oil)

Optional Additions:

  • Heat: Thai bird's eye chillies, red peeper flakes, or chili garlic sauce
  • Exta depth: Grated fresh ginger or a splash of oyster sauce
  • Garnish: Fresh cilantro, mint, and lime wedges

Preparation Process

  1. Prepare the chicken

    • Cut the chicken into bite-sized pieces or thin strips
    • If using chicken breasts, try to cut them into similar sized pieces so they cook evenly

  2. Prpeare the lemongrass

    • Remove the tough outer layers from the lemongrass
    • Cut off the dry green tops
    • Use the tender white/light green portion
    • Finely mince it - the finer the better

  3. Make the marinade

    • Add the minced lemongrass, garlic, green onion, fish sauce, soy sauce, brown sugar or honey, lime juice, and oil to a bowl
    • Add chili and ginger if using
    • Add oyster sauce if you want a deeper, slightly sweeter flavor
    • Mix everything thoroughly

  4. Marinate the chicken

    • Add the chicken to the marinade
    • Toss until every piece is well coated
    • Cover and refrigerate for at least 30 minutes
    • For stronger flavor, marinate for 2-4 hours

  5. Heat your pan

    • Heat a large frying pan or skillet over medium-high heat

  6. Cook the chicken

    • Add the chicken in a single layer
    • Don't overcrowd the pan - cook in batches if necessary
    • Let the chicken sit for 2-3 minutes before stirring so it develops browning

  7. Brown and caramelize

    • Continue cooking for about 6-10 minutes, turning occasionally
    • The sugar/honey and lemongrass should caramelize slightly and create browned edges
    • Make sure the chicken is fully cooked through

  8. Check the chicken

    • Chicken should reach an internal temperature of 165 degrees Farenheit at the thickest part

  9. Finish with lime

    • Turn off the heat
    • Taste the chicken
    • Add another squeeze of frsh lime juice if you want it brighter and more acidic

  10. Garnish and serve

    • Top with fresh cilantro and/or mint
    • Serve with lime wedges
    • It's especially good over jasmine rice, alongside vegetables, or in a rice/noddle bowl

ENJOY!!!
