Recipe for Paneer Tikka Masala

Brief Description

Tikka masala is a popular curry dish made with roasted, grilled paneer cheese simmered in a creamy, spiced tomato sauce.

Recipe Elements

Paneer Tikka: Firm Cubes of Paneer (Indian Cottage cheese) that are marinated in thick yogurt, ginger-garlic paste, lemon juice, and spices like garam masala, tumeric, and chili powder.

Charred Veggies The paneer is usually skewered or pan-seared alongside chunky pieces of bell peppers (capsicum), onions, and tomatoes until lightly charred.

Masala Gravy: The sauce features a smooth base of pureed or sautéed tomatoes and onions, simmered with warm spices, cream or butter, and crushed dried fenugreek leaves (kasuri methi) for an aromatic finish.

Ingredients

Paneer Tikka Marinade

  • 250 grams paneer (cubed)
  • 1/2 cup hung curd or thick greek yogurt
  • 1 teaspoon ginger-garlic paste
  • 1/2 teaspoon Kashmiri red chili powder
  • 1/2 garam masala
  • 1/4 kasuri methi (dried fenugreek leaves
  • 1 tablespoon gram flour (besan, roasted) or cornstarch
  • 1 teaspoon oil
  • 1/2 small onion and 1/2 small bell pepper (diced)

Masala Gravy

  • 2 tablespoon oil or butter
  • 1 medium onion (finely chopped or blended into a paste
  • 2 to 3 medium tomatoes (pureed or finely chopped
  • 1 teaspoon ginger-garllic paste
  • 1 teaspoon coriander powder
  • 1/2 teaspoon cumin powder
  • 1/2 garam masala
  • 1/2 teaspoon Kashmiri red chilli powder
  • 1/4 teaspoon tumeric powder
  • 3 to 4 tablespoons fresh cream
  • 1 teaspoon kasuri methi
  • Salt and sugar (to taste)

Preparation Steps

Prepare the paneer/ Make the marinade

  1. Cut 250 grams paneer into medium-sized cubes
  2. Dice 1/2 small onion and 1/2 small bell pepper
  3. add 1/2 cup hung curd or thick greek yogurt to a bowl
  4. add 1 tsp ginger-garlic paste
  5. add 1/2 tsp Kashmiri red chili powder
  6. add 1/2 tsp garam masala
  7. add 1/4 tsp kasuri methi
  8. add 1 tbsp roasted besan or cornstarch
  9. add salt to taste
  10. add 1tsp oil and mix everything together

Marinate the paneer

  1. add the paneer cubes, dice onion, and bell pepper to the marinade
  2. Gently mix until everything is well coated
  3. Let it marinate for 20-30 minutes

Cook the Paneer

  1. Heat a pan over medium-high heat
  2. Add the marianted paneer, onion, and bell pepper
  3. Cook for 5-8 minutes, turn occasionally, until the paneer is lightly browned and the vegetables have softened
  4. Remove from the pan and set aside

Start the masala gravy

  1. Heat 2 tbsp oil or butter in the same pan
  2. Add finely chopped or blended onion
  3. Cook for 5-7 minutes until softened and lightly golden

Add the ginger and tomatoes

  1. Add 1 tsp ginger-garlic paste and cook for about 1 minute
  2. Add the pureed or finely chopped tomatoes
  3. Cook for 8-10 minutes,stirring occasionally, until the tomatoes break down and the oil begins to separate from the mixture

Add the spices

  1. Add 1/2 tsp coriander powder
  2. Add 1/2 tsp cumin powder
  3. Add 1/2 garam masala
  4. Add 1/2 tsp Kashmiri red chili powder
  5. Add 1/4 tsp tumeric powder
  6. Add salt to taste
  7. Stir and cook for 1-2 minutes

Make the gravy creamy

  1. Add 3-4 tbsp fresh cream
  2. Stir well
  3. Add a small pinch of sugar if the tomatoes are to acidic
  4. If the gravy is to thick, add a small splash of water and simmer for a few minutes

Add the Paneer

  1. Add the cooked paneer, onion, and bell pepper to the gravy
  2. Gently stir everything together
  3. Simmer for 3-5 minutes on low heat

Finish the dish

  1. Crush 1 tsp kasuri methi between your fingers and sprinkle it into the gravy
  2. Taste and adjust the salt, sugar, pr spices
  3. Serve hot with rice, naan, roti, or chapati
